About GDGOC

Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGOC) is a university-based community group for students interested in Google developer technologies.

Through our community, students can learn new skills, collaborate on innovative projects, and grow professionally by connecting with peers and tech leaders.

Event Highlights

  • Introduction to GDGOC

    An insightful opening session introducing the vision, mission, and roadmap for the chapter at COMSATS Attock.

  • Tech Talks & Expert Insights

    Engaging discussions on modern development trends, cloud technologies, and open-source contribution.

  • Interactive Team Networking

    Students interacted with core team leads and joined various domain sub-teams based on their passions.

GDGOC Teams

  • Web Team: Focuses on building responsive web applications, websites, and mastering frontend/backend technologies.
  • App Team: Explores mobile app development using modern frameworks like Flutter and native tools.
  • Media Team: Captures moments, edits videos, and manages creative visual content for events.
  • PR Team: Handles public relations, outreach, and external communications.
  • AI/ML Team: Investigates artificial intelligence, machine learning models, and data science concepts.
  • Management Team: Coordinates event logistics, planning, and smooth execution of campus activities.
  • Women in Tech Team: Empowers and supports female students in technology domains and leadership.
